Kopi Senja, a new grab-and-go kopi susu kiosk next to the Blok M MRT exit in South Jakarta.
Es kopi susu gula aren with fresh milk, Rp22.000 a cup. Less-sugar option on request.
Open 6.30 to 21.00. Pay at the counter with QRIS, or order on GoFood and GrabFood.
Opening week: buy one get one, weekdays 7.00 to 9.00.
